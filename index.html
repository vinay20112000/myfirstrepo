Meet Our Residents

From the African savanna to the Amazon rainforest — discover the incredible animals that call WildVista home.

African Lion

Panthera leo

The king of the savanna. Our pride of five lions roams a sprawling, sun-drenched enclosure designed to mimic the Serengeti grasslands.

👁 Popular Exhibit

Bengal Tiger

Panthera tigris tigris

Witness the power and grace of the world's most iconic big cat in our lush, jungle-themed Tiger Trail habitat.

⚠ Endangered

African Elephant

Loxodonta africana

Meet our gentle giants. The Elephant Oasis features mud wallows, a waterfall, and plenty of space to roam.

🌊 Fan Favorite

Red Panda

Ailurus fulgens

One of the most beloved animals at the zoo. Watch these agile climbers navigate their bamboo-filled haven with playful curiosity.

⚠ Endangered

Reticulated Giraffe

Giraffa reticulata

Stand eye-to-eye with the tallest land animal on Earth at our elevated Giraffe Feeding Platform.

🥤 Feeding Available

Humboldt Penguin

Spheniscus humboldti

Dive into the Penguin Coast — a temperature-controlled habitat with underwater viewing tunnels and daily feeding shows.

Unforgettable Experiences

Go beyond watching — touch, feed, and connect with wildlife in ways you never imagined.

🥤 Giraffe Feeding

Hand-feed lettuce to our friendly giraffes from an elevated platform — a magical moment for all ages.

👓 Keeper for a Day

Shadow a zookeeper, prepare animal diets, and learn what it takes to care for over 200 species.

🌙 Twilight Safari

Explore the zoo after dark with guided night walks. Hear the roar of lions and the howl of wolves under the stars.
